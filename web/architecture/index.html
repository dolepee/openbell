TRUST MODEL

AI proposes.
The contract disposes.

OpenBell keeps intelligence and authority separate: the model evaluates risk, signatures authorize exact terms, and immutable contract limits determine what can execute.

END-TO-END CONTROL FLOW

One receivable, five enforceable boundaries.

  1. 01

    Invoice authority

    Supplier and payer sign the same EIP-712 invoice hash. The document remains offchain while its identity and terms become tamper-evident.

    2 SIGNERS
  2. 02

    Risk authority

    A strictly parsed model response returns REJECT or bounded advance and fee terms. The first genuine response is authoritative.

    AI DECISION
  3. 03

    Contract authority

    The effective advance is the minimum of the supplier request, model ceiling, and immutable contract ceiling.

    MIN(REQUEST, AI, CODE)
  4. 04

    Funding authority

    A funder chooses one approved invoice and transfers the exact advance. There is no pooled liquidity or automatic custody.

    DIRECT FUNDER
  5. 05

    Settlement authority

    The payer settles the exact amount due directly to the funder. Terminal state prevents duplicate execution.

    FINAL STATE

ONCHAIN INVARIANTS

Small surface. Hard guarantees.

01

Dual authorization

No unilateral invoice creation. Supplier and payer signatures must recover against one canonical digest.

02

Bounded risk terms

Advance and fee cannot exceed immutable contract limits even when a model or signer proposes more.

03

Duplicate resistance

Invoice identifiers, document hashes, signer nonces, and decision nonces cannot be replayed.

04

Exact accounting

Funding and settlement assert exact token deltas and explicit terminal invoice states.

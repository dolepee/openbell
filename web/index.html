THE WHOLE PRODUCT

AI assesses. The underwriter authorizes. X Layer enforces.

A supplier does not need to understand hashes, model plumbing, or contract calls to understand who controls the money. The receipt-bound pilot shows how a settlement can inform a later assessment through a verified, published checkpoint.

  1. 01

    Parties set the terms.

    Supplier and payer sign the same invoice package and document commitment.

    REQUEST
  2. 02

    AI assesses; the underwriter authorizes.

    The connected service seals one Bankr-mediated GPT-5.6 Terra response. The configured underwriter may sign bounded terms or preserve a refusal; neither can transfer funds.

    DECISION
  3. 03

    X Layer enforces the hard cap.

    A funder can execute only an underwriter-signed advance that stays within the contract's immutable 80% ceiling.

    CAP

Exact trust boundary: the contract verifies the authorized signer, invoice-bound hashes, terms, freshness, expiry, and immutable ceilings. It does not independently attest remote model execution.

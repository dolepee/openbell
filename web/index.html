THE PRODUCT LOOP

From signed invoice to exact settlement.

OpenBell separates who supplies information, who evaluates risk, and who can move money. No participant can silently assume another role.

  1. 01

    Sign the receivable

    Supplier and payer sign the same invoice terms and document hash through EIP-712.

  2. 02

    Assess confirmed history

    The model receives receipt-derived X Layer history, not supplier-declared performance.

  3. 03

    Fund exact terms

    A funder advances only the smallest amount authorized by the request, model, and contract.

  4. 04

    Settle directly

    The payer repays the fixed amount to the recorded funder and the invoice reaches SETTLED.

BOUNDED AUTHORITY

Intelligence evaluates. Signatures authorize. Code enforces.

Supplier + payer

Bind the invoice.

Both parties sign identical terms. The document stays offchain; its hash is bound to the deal.

AI underwriter

Tighten or refuse.

The model can lower an advance, increase the permitted fee, or reject. It cannot move funds.

Human exception

Accept less, visibly.

After refusal, a disclosed operator may sign stricter terms while preserving the rejected artifact.

X Layer contract

Enforce the minimum.

Execution resolves to the smallest signed and permitted amount, with exact token accounting.
